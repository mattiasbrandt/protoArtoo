The question this exists to settle

Flip Loop as authored to Loop expanded. The same fourteen seconds, drawn two ways.

Authored keeps the one object a builder made — a 4-beat bar, its period, its repeat count — and it is the only view where re-timing the routine is one edit. Expanded shows all 182 panel commands the droid actually receives, which is what a card list shows today.

ADR 0046 says a sequence stores what the builder meant. If that holds, authored is the editing view and expanded is a read-only check.

A block is a span, not an event

Every panel command in DM:CANTINA is instantaneous — :OPP1 at one millisecond, :CLP1 at another. The block between them is derived, by pairing open with close on the same Part.

That pairing is the whole argument for a timeline. A card list can show you 26 commands; only this can show you that PP1 is open for 923 ms and PP2 is open for the other half of the bar.

It also makes :OF flutter visibly different — it has no defined end, so it can never draw a span.

What it deliberately does not do

Read-only. No dragging a block, no resizing, no inspector, no snapping. Specific 1 said land read-only first, and this is that.

No Gesture appears here either — DM:CANTINA predates ADR 0046 and is hand-written as 26 individual commands. A Gesture would be one block spanning these thirteen lanes, and that is the next thing worth drawing.

Where the data comes from. The steps are the real ones from src/tasks/sequence_catalog.cpp:242 — SEQ_LOOP(100, 26, 1846, 14000) over a 26-step body at relative 0 ms and 923 ms. 1846 ms is four beats at 130 BPM and 923 ms is two, which is where the beat grid comes from rather than a number anyone typed. Part names, shorthand, bearings and control: state are read from docs/droid-parts.yaml.

Colour follows #327. Amber and red are reserved for you can act on this and this is stopped or refused, so neither appears on a block or a lane — teal here is structure and a cursor, never state. Dark only, one palette, no light theme.

Not connected to a droid. Nothing on this page talks to a controller. The dome drawing is commanded intent inferred from the sequence, which is the same claim ADR 0045 lets the model make — the dome does not report its panel positions back.
