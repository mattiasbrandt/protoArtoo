DFPlayer Mini

Not built yet. Nothing to do here.

This picks which module the body talks to, not which sounds it has. Those stay on the module's card. Takes effect after the controller restarts; the finish step says when. This board has one serial port left over and two things that want it, so while the dome is talking the module cannot be asked what is playing and the plate says so: SOUND LINK · DOME HAS BUS. Playing a sound is never held up. This board has a serial port to spare, so the module can be asked what is playing whatever the dome is doing.
