Your droid

Finding out what your droid is doing...

Every part on one picture, so you can see what is open, what is closed and what nothing drives without reading rows one at a time. Pick a part on the drawing and the panel beside it names that part and what you can do with it.

What you see is what the controller told each part, never a reading taken from it: nothing on this droid can feel where a servo really is, so a jammed part draws exactly what a free one does. A part nothing is driving shows no position at all rather than a dimmed one, because a dimmed mark is still a claim. Parts with no place on the droid - the spare slots - are not on the picture and are in the table below.

Finding out what drives each part...

Pick an output to wire a part. A part is only ever on one output, so picking a new one moves it, and this page asks first before it takes a part off the output it is on. One output can drive several parts that always move together, like two doors on one lead.

A part nothing drives yet carries Find by moving: the droid twitches each spare output a little, one at a time, about wherever it sits, and you press That one when the part moves. Stop ends the run at any moment, and so does the estop.
