Finding out what drives each part...

Pick an output to wire a part. A part is only ever on one output, so picking a new one moves it, and this page asks first before it takes a part off the output it is on. One output can drive several parts that always move together, like two doors on one lead.

A part nothing drives yet carries Find by moving: the droid twitches each spare output a little, one at a time, about wherever it sits, and you press That one when the part moves. Stop ends the run at any moment, and so does the estop.
