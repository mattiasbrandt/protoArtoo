PCB Serial Legend

  • S0 - ESP debug
  • S1 - Hoverboard
  • S2 - Sound
  • S3 - Dome Control

Transport Map

  • S0 / UART0 - USB debug on GPIO 1 TX / GPIO 3 RX at 115200
  • S1 / UART1 - Hoverboard drive link on GPIO 16 TX / GPIO 17 RX at 115200
  • S2 / Sound - Audio transport on GPIO 26 TX / GPIO 35 RX at 9600
  • S3 / UART2 - Dome Control link on GPIO 33 TX / GPIO 34 RX at 9600
  • CH1 / CH2 - SBUS inputs on GPIO 15 / GPIO 13 via RMT, not serial UARTs

Current Phase Reality

  • USB debug on S0 is active now and used for firmware logs.
  • Hoverboard transport on S1 is implemented in firmware, but full hardware behavior needs the Artoo PCB + hoverboard chain connected.
  • Sound transport on S2 is electrically defined, but full audio behavior belongs to later-phase hardware work.
  • Dome Control on S3 is the traced body-dome serial path, but full heartbeat/command behavior belongs to later phases.

Known Facts from Pin Map

  • Earlier assumptions that S3 was hoverboard were wrong; traced PCB legend confirms S1 = Hoverboard.
  • GPIO 34 and GPIO 35 are input-only, so they are valid RX pins but cannot be repurposed as TX outputs.
  • S2 RX exists physically, so future audio drivers may use module responses if needed.
