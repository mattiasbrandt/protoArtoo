🔌 About Serial Ports

Check this page when troubleshooting serial connections. The Connection Status section shows whether each port is active. If a port shows as down, verify its enable toggle in Setup and check the wiring at the labelled PCB header. S0 (USB debug) is always active and requires no configuration.

🔌 Serial Port Overview

Hardware serial connections on the Artoo Controller PCB.
  • S0 - USB Debug (always active)
  • S1 - Hoverboard Drive Motors
  • S2 - Sound Module (DY-SV5W)
  • S3 - Dome Controller Link
