Drive controls

hold to drive, release to stop

Hold a direction and the droid keeps going; let go and it stops. If commands stop arriving the droid stops on its own after the web control timeout set below, so a browser that freezes does not leave the feet running.

Drive settings

saved on the droid as you change them

The speed cap and the three preset limits the buttons above swap between. Every change here is written to the droid as you make it, so there is nothing to press afterwards.

Each preset is a speed cap the buttons at the top of this page jump to. Give them three different numbers: two presets sharing a number cannot be told apart once one is applied.

Preset limits must be distinct. Duplicate values make preset switching ambiguous.
