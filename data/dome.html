Dome ESC

saved on the droid as you change them

The pulse widths the controller drives the dome's speed controller between, and the share of that range it is allowed to use. Every change here is written to the droid as you make it.

The ESC's own calibration, its PWM frequency and its voltage cutoff are set from the ISD Go app over Bluetooth, not from here. These four numbers only decide how wide a pulse the controller sends it.
